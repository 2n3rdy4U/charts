Each CSV opens with '#' comment lines giving its source, definition, the month it runs through,
the page it belongs to and the citation to use. pandas.read_csv(url, comment='#') reads past them.
Rates are fractions (0.05 = 5%). Files are rewritten in place as new data arrive; the 'Data through'
line identifies a vintage.
