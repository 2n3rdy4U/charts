The numbers behind the charts, as CSV

Each file is written from the same figures the charts draw, every time the data update, and opens with a few comment lines that say what it is, where it comes from, the month it runs through and how to cite it. Rates are fractions (0.05 is 5%). The methodology defines every series.

Licence and citation

The files are published under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0): use, share and build on them freely, for any purpose, with attribution to Consumer Credit Matters. Each file's Cite as line gives the form: Consumer Credit Matters, “dataset”, data through month, file address. The ABS figures are compiled from public filings with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (Forms ABS-EE and 10-D). The macro file carries the dashboard's series whose publishers are US government bodies, the Federal Reserve or others that allow redistribution; those figures remain their publishers' and should be credited to them as the source column names them. A few series the Macro page shows under their owners' terms are not in the file; the file's header lists them.

Links to a file address are welcome. The files are rewritten in place as new data arrive, so a cited “data through” month identifies the vintage. Changes to a definition or to coverage are dated on the changelog.
