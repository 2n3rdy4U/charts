At pricing — terms from the deal's prospectus and pricing supplement; they do not update.
Credit Score — weighted-average obligor credit score of the pool at cut-off, as stated in the prospectus.
APR — the pool's weighted-average APR at cut-off, as its prospectus states it (the summary and the pool table agree); for deals before 2017, as the issuer's later prospectuses print it.
Hard CE — initial hard credit enhancement for the senior notes, the total the rating agencies quote: Sub (all rated classes below the AAA notes; an unrated residual Class N is not subordination) + Reserve (the reserve fund at closing) + OC (overcollateralization at closing, net of any yield supplement), each as a % of the initial pool balance less any yield supplement — the adjusted pool the issuers and rating agencies measure enhancement against. Excess spread is not included. The total is blank where a part is not known.
Latest reported — from the most recent monthly servicer report (SEC Form 10-D). 60+ DQ is the share of loans 60+ days past due in the ABS-EE loan tape at its latest period; CNL is cumulative net loss as a % of the original pool balance; Pool Factor is the current pool balance ÷ original.
PSR marks a deal with a presale report (2024 deals onward); it opens from the deal name.
Credit Score for deals before 2017 is the pool's weighted average at cut-off, as the issuer's later prospectuses print it.
Hard CE for deals priced 2017–2020 is read from each deal's final prospectus, every part checked against its first servicer report or a second figure in the prospectus. Those prospectuses do not print ratings, so the AAA notes are the Class A notes — the same classes as on every deal since 2021.
