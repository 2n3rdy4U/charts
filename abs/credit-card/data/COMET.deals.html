At pricing — terms from the series' prospectus and pricing supplement; they do not update. A credit-card master trust issues notes in series against one revolving pool of receivables, so the trust's performance (the charts) applies to every series it has outstanding.
Expected maturity — the expected principal payment date of the senior class; a series is matured once that date has passed. A coupon or rating shown as — was not captured from the prospectus; the filing itself states it.
PSR marks a series with a presale report (2024 series onward); it opens from the series name.
