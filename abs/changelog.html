Changelog

What changed in the figures and the pages · through October 13, 2026

A definition that changed, a series added or retired, coverage that grew, a page that moved. The methodology describes every figure as it stands today; this page is the record of how it got there. Daily data updates are not listed: figures change whenever an issuer files, and each page says the month it runs through.

  • Oct 13, 2026
    Site

    Accessibility pass

    Every page checked against WCAG 2.1 AA with an automated audit on desktop and phone, and walked with the keyboard. Text colours meet the contrast rule, every control has a name and a visible focus ring, charts carry a description and the Table display is their text form, pickers and menus close on Escape.

  • Oct 13, 2026
    Site

    Data downloads

    Every series on the site can be downloaded as a CSV file with its source, definition and citation in the header, under a CC BY 4.0 licence.

  • Oct 12, 2026
    Credit Card

    Full history for every trust, tied back to the certificates

    Each trust's figures now run from its first public servicer certificate. Yield and Excess Spread are separate tabs. The methodology lists what each trust's figure is.

  • Oct 12, 2026
    Macro

    The address is the view

    The category, series, transform and date range are in the page address, so a link opens the view it was copied from. The page is dated by its newest observation.

  • Oct 12, 2026
    Issuance

    Filters in the address

    Metric, period, sector, rating and agency filters are in the page address; the page is dated by its newest filing.

  • Oct 9, 2026
    Credit Card

    Credit card performance pages

    One page per master trust and an All Trusts series weighted by receivables, with delinquency, charge-offs, payment rate, yield, excess spread, balance and each trust's deals. The old /cc/ address forwards.

  • Oct 9, 2026
    Auto Loan

    The Explorer retired; its addresses forward

    Every old Explorer link opens the matching performance page. Year-over-year view, an All Issuers line, repossession counts and the Porsche lease shelf added.

  • Oct 1, 2026
    Auto Loan

    New performance pages for auto loan and auto lease shelves

    One page per shelf with delinquency, losses, repossessions, vintages and a deals table; lease shelves show credit net loss and residual value gain or loss from the servicer reports.

  • Sep 29, 2026
    Auto Loan

    First-time repossession rate

    Repossessions in the month over the loans at risk at its start, from the loan-level filings. The Credit Score Analysis tab is retired.

  • Sep 23, 2026
    Newsletter

    Archive search

    Every past briefing is searchable from the masthead of the newsletter pages.

  • Jul 18, 2026
    Auto Loan

    Losses from the servicer reports

    Loss curves and annualized net loss now come from the monthly 10-D servicer reports, reconciled deal by deal, instead of being reconstructed from the loan-level files.

  • Jul 9, 2026
    Issuance

    Deal size from the class table

    A deal's size is the sum of its class principals as the prospectus lists them.

  • Jun 12, 2026
    Macro

    CPI breakdown table

    The CPI table shows each component's weight and its contribution, with a heat map of the monthly changes.

  • Apr 7, 2026
    Macro

    Recession shading

    NBER recession periods can be shaded on every macro chart.
