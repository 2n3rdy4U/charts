Changelog

What changed in the figures and the pages · through October 29, 2026

A definition that changed, a series added or retired, coverage that grew, a page that moved. The methodology describes every figure as it stands today; this page is the record of how it got there. Daily data updates are not listed: figures change whenever an issuer files, and each page says the month it runs through.

  • Oct 29, 2026
    Auto Loan

    The performance page opens on All Issuers

    The Auto Loan and Auto Lease performance pages now open on the market series by calendar month rather than on one issuer's shelf (Carvana for loans, Ford Credit for leases). Pick issuers from the picker as before; every deep link to a named shelf still opens that shelf.

  • Oct 27, 2026
    Issuance

    Honda's yield supplement is not shown as YSOC

    Honda supplements the yield on its low-rate loans with a cash account deposited at closing, not with yield supplement overcollateralization, so the YSOC row of its presale reports and deal comparisons now reads n.a. On eight Honda deals it had shown the account's deposit as a share of the pool (5.71% for 2025-1). Honda's enhancement figures, which never counted the account, are unchanged.

  • Oct 26, 2026
    Auto Loan

    Hard credit enhancement for captive lenders' deals, corrected

    A correction to the Hard CE columns of the Deals tables and the enhancement figures on the presale reports for deals with a yield supplement (Ford, Toyota, Hyundai, GM, Nissan, World Omni, Capital One Prime, Fifth Third and others). Initial overcollateralization is now what the deal holds at closing - for Ford and Toyota, zero, as their prospectuses state - where it had shown the level the deal builds to (2.00% for Ford, 0.85% for Toyota, 3.00% for Hyundai). Every part of the total is now measured against the pool net of the yield supplement, as the issuers and rating agencies measure it, and the yield supplement shown is the amount at closing; on 40 deals it had been the preliminary prospectus's figure. Ford's typical Hard CE moves from 6.89% to 5.25% and Toyota's from 3.46% to 2.75%. Lease deals and lenders without a yield supplement are unchanged.

  • Oct 25, 2026
    Credit Card

    Credit card history back to 1996, and earlier trusts

    The credit card trusts' records now reach back as far as their servicer reports allow - to 1996 for American Express, Citi, Capital One and Bank of America, 2002 for Chase and 2004 for Comenity - and ten trusts no longer issuing (among them Discover, Sears, Providian, Metris, Household and National City) are listed under Earlier trusts. Earlier months use each measure's present definition; where an older report printed only dollar amounts, the rate is calculated and the methodology says which. All Trusts now runs from 1996 for delinquency and from 2008 for charge-offs. A correction - All Trusts figures for August 2009 to November 2012 had left out Bank of America, the largest trust, whose reports for those months were missing; they now include it.

  • Oct 25, 2026
    Issuance

    Presale reports lead with enhancement beneath the AAA notes

    The structure block on every auto loan and lease presale report now opens with the enhancement beneath the AAA term notes (subordinate notes, overcollateralization and reserve, initial and at target) with its components beneath it, then pricing and terms. "AAA % of pool" is retired. The AAA spread is now the fixed-rate AAA class nearest a two-year weighted-average life, with the class and its WAL shown; floating-rate classes are no longer used. The tranche table shows each class's enhancement, coupon and the benchmark its spread is quoted over, and marks classes the depositor retained. The same rows appear in the Performance pages' deal comparisons.

  • Oct 24, 2026
    Credit Card

    American Express net charge-offs from 2006

    American Express's net charge-off rate now runs from June 2006, calculated from the defaults and recoveries in its servicer reports exactly as Amex calculates the net rate it has printed since October 2008. Months already shown are unchanged.

  • Oct 24, 2026
    Credit Card

    Excess spread as the early-amortization trigger tests it

    The Excess Spread chart now opens on the 3-month average that each trust's early-amortization trigger tests, measured where the trigger applies - the whole issuance series for Chase, Citi, Bank of America, Capital One, Synchrony and Wells Fargo, and the weakest series for American Express, First National, Comenity and Barclays Dryrock, named in the tooltip. Capital One, which reports its trigger in dollars, now has a percentage, calculated on its prospectus's definition. The Monthly view keeps the trust-level figure, and All Trusts' monthly excess spread now runs from 2006.

  • Oct 23, 2026
    Credit Card

    Earlier charge-off and delinquency rates for five trusts

    Charge-off rates now run from 2006-2011 for Chase, Capital One (net), Bank of America and Comenity, and 30+ day delinquency from 2006 for Amex and 2009 for Comenity. Where an older report printed only dollar amounts, the rate is computed on the site's definitions and used only where it matched the trust's own printed rate in later months; the methodology says which. Months already shown are unchanged.

  • Oct 23, 2026
    Credit Card

    Chase delinquency from 2007, Barclays Dryrock gross losses from 2012

    Chase's delinquency now runs from October 2007 and Barclays Dryrock's gross loss rate from December 2012, read from the same lines of the trusts' servicer reports in the layout they used before November 2015. Months already shown are unchanged.

  • Oct 23, 2026
    Auto Loan

    Deal terms for deals priced 2017 to 2020

    The Deals tables now show closing date, size, weighted-average credit score and (for loans) APR for 221 auto loan and lease deals priced from 2017 to 2020, read from each deal's final prospectus. Each figure is shown only where an independent record confirms it, the deal's servicer reports, its closing filing or its loan-level data, so a few cells stay blank. Credit enhancement at pricing is not yet shown for these deals.

  • Oct 23, 2026
    Issuance

    Class sizes and credit scores corrected on recent auto deals

    Class sizes on 10 auto deals from 2021 on now match each deal's final prospectus and first servicer report; four of those deals' sizes change (Capital One Prime 2024-1, Santander Drive 2021-2, Toyota 2024-A, Ford Credit 2024-B), and subordination and hard credit enhancement change where a class below AAA was resized. The weighted-average credit score changes on 32 deals, mostly BMW, Capital One Prime and Nissan lease, to the figure the prospectus states for the deal's own pool, which its loan-level data confirms.

  • Oct 23, 2026
    Auto Lease

    Deals that stopped reporting count as wound down

    A deal whose servicer reports stopped more than three months before its issuer's newest report now counts as wound down, whatever its last pool factor. Lease trusts are usually called while 12-23% of the pool remains, so 64 lease deals (and one Santander loan deal) had been counted as active years after their last report; issuers' active-deal counts fall accordingly. In every Deals table, a wound-down deal's latest-reported figures are now marked with the month of its final report.

  • Oct 23, 2026
    Auto Loan

    Pool APR in the Deals table

    Each loan issuer's Deals table now shows the pool's weighted-average APR at cut-off, as the deal's final prospectus states it. Deals before 2017 show it where the issuer's later prospectuses print it and it has been checked against the record; elsewhere the cell is blank.

  • Oct 23, 2026
    Issuance

    Pool APR corrected on auto presale reports

    The weighted-average APR shown for auto loan deals now matches the pool figure in each deal's final prospectus. For some issuers it previously showed the new-vehicle or non-subvented figure, or an earlier pool's, so the APR in comparison tables changes for many deals, by more than half a point for 86.

  • Oct 22, 2026
    Auto Loan

    Earlier issuers, and choosing vintages

    The issuer picker lists issuers that no longer issue public auto ABS (Triad, United PanAm, Long Beach Acceptance, Franklin, Bank of America, Huntington, Chrysler Financial) with their loss curves by deal age, and Ally's and BMW's loss curves for every loan originated each year. Vintage charts open on the last ten years of vintages; the Vintages control shows the last five, all, or any span of years.

  • Oct 22, 2026
    Auto Loan

    Loss curves back to 2001

    The Losses tab now includes earlier deals for most issuers, back to 2001 where the record allows. Deals from 2010 to 2016 come from the trusts' own monthly servicer reports; earlier deals from the static-pool tables issuers publish in their prospectuses. With many deals on a chart, lines are colored by vintage year.

  • Oct 22, 2026
    Credit Card

    Yield before losses, and excess spread for the whole trust

    Portfolio yield is now shown before charge-offs for every trust (most trusts previously showed it net), so trusts compare like for like and charge-offs stay on their own line. Excess spread is the trust's own figure, or the average across its series weighted by size where each series reports separately; a series in its first month is left out. The methodology lists each trust's source line.

  • Oct 20, 2026
    Auto Lease

    Credit gains shown on every lease shelf

    A net credit gain now shows as a negative loss on every lease shelf, monthly and cumulative. Some shelves previously showed such months as zero, so their credit-loss history now dips below zero where the servicer reported a gain.

  • Oct 19, 2026
    Auto Lease

    Residual results by month, and All Issuers for leases

    The Residuals tab's time series shows each shelf's gain or loss on the leased vehicles sold each month, as a share of their residual value. The lease pages gain an All Issuers line for delinquency and credit losses.

  • Oct 13, 2026
    Auto Lease

    Turn-in rate

    The lease pages show each shelf's turn-in rate, the share of leases reaching their scheduled end whose vehicle comes back rather than being bought, by month and by deal age, for the five servicers that report one. The methodology says how each servicer states it.

  • Oct 13, 2026
    Site

    Accessibility pass

    Every page checked against WCAG 2.1 AA with an automated audit on desktop and phone, and walked with the keyboard. Text colours meet the contrast rule, every control has a name and a visible focus ring, charts carry a description and the Table display is their text form, pickers and menus close on Escape.

  • Oct 13, 2026
    Site

    Data downloads

    Every series on the site can be downloaded as a CSV file with its source, definition and citation in the header, under a CC BY 4.0 licence.

  • Oct 12, 2026
    Credit Card

    Full history for every trust, tied back to the certificates

    Each trust's figures now run from its first public servicer certificate. Yield and Excess Spread are separate tabs. The methodology lists what each trust's figure is.

  • Oct 12, 2026
    Macro

    The address is the view

    The category, series, transform and date range are in the page address, so a link opens the view it was copied from. The page is dated by its newest observation.

  • Oct 12, 2026
    Issuance

    Filters in the address

    Metric, period, sector, rating and agency filters are in the page address; the page is dated by its newest filing.

  • Oct 9, 2026
    Credit Card

    Credit card performance pages

    One page per master trust and an All Trusts series weighted by receivables, with delinquency, charge-offs, payment rate, yield, excess spread, balance and each trust's deals. The old /cc/ address forwards.

  • Oct 9, 2026
    Auto Loan

    The Explorer retired; its addresses forward

    Every old Explorer link opens the matching performance page. Year-over-year view, an All Issuers line, repossession counts and the Porsche lease shelf added.

  • Oct 1, 2026
    Auto Loan

    New performance pages for auto loan and auto lease shelves

    One page per shelf with delinquency, losses, repossessions, vintages and a deals table; lease shelves show credit net loss and residual value gain or loss from the servicer reports.

  • Sep 29, 2026
    Auto Loan

    First-time repossession rate

    Repossessions in the month over the loans at risk at its start, from the loan-level filings. The Credit Score Analysis tab is retired.

  • Sep 23, 2026
    Newsletter

    Archive search

    Every past briefing is searchable from the masthead of the newsletter pages.

  • Jul 18, 2026
    Auto Loan

    Losses from the servicer reports

    Loss curves and annualized net loss now come from the monthly 10-D servicer reports, reconciled deal by deal, instead of being reconstructed from the loan-level files.

  • Jul 9, 2026
    Issuance

    Deal size from the class table

    A deal's size is the sum of its class principals as the prospectus lists them.

  • Jun 12, 2026
    Macro

    CPI breakdown table

    The CPI table shows each component's weight and its contribution, with a heat map of the monthly changes.

  • Apr 7, 2026
    Macro

    Recession shading

    NBER recession periods can be shaded on every macro chart.
