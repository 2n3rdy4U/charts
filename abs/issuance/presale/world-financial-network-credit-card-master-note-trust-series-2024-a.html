Methodology & Sources

Capital structure (class size, coupon / spread, WAL, ratings) is sourced from the issuer’s pricing FWP and 424B5 prospectus supplement on EDGAR. Trust-level performance (Monthly Payment Rate, Net Charge-Off, Portfolio Yield, Excess Spread, delinquency) is computed from the trust’s monthly 10-D distribution reports. Subordination and AAA Sub are derived from class principals against the series invested amount; CC master trusts have no fixed pool balance, so all percentages in the Capital Structure are notes-basis. Peer cohorts are sliced to the subject’s closing date so the snapshot reflects what was knowable at issuance.
