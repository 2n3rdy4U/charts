Methodology & data lineage
Sources
FWP = Free Writing Prospectus pricing table (class name, principal, coupon/spread, rating, benchmark). 424B5 = prospectus supplement (pool composition, credit enhancement, cut-off date, legal final). ABS-EE = monthly 10-D loan-level reports (per-series CNL, DQ).
Capital-Structure footnotes
a FWP pricing table  ·  b Derived (principal ÷ initial pool balance from 424B5; reverts to principal ÷ total notes when pool balance isn't extracted)  ·  c 424B5 prospectus
Comparison-table conventions
Row-label color indicates source of record (same key as above). Subject-column cells are annotated with a compact signed delta vs. peer median — green when the subject sits on the favorable side of its peer set, red when unfavorable; hover for context.
AAA spreads reflect market conditions at each deal's pricing date; multi-bp differences between deals priced months apart may reflect rate-environment drift rather than credit fundamentals.
Peer cohort
Subject’s sponsor-type (captive / bank / IFC / online) and FICO tier (prime ≥720, near-prime 640–719, subprime <640), with a fallback chain when the strict cohort is too thin. “Representative” peer set = one most-recent deal per peer ticker, capped at five columns.
Historical performance
CNL = cumulative charge-off $ ÷ original pool $, computed per-series from ABS-EE loan-level reports via generate_series_loss_data.py.
